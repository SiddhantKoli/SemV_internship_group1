Title: Historical fish prices for the Thane and Palghar district group
Source: Directorate of Fisheries, Government of Maharashtra, Statistical Abstract of Maharashtra 2022-23, Table 16.6
URL: https://mahades.maharashtra.gov.in/files/publication/StatisticalAbstractofMaharashtraState2022-23.pdf
Checked: 2026-11-05

The 2022-23 Maharashtra Statistical Abstract reports average annual wholesale boatside marine fish prices in rupees per tonne. Its geographic column is combined Thane / Palghar; it is not Palghar-only and not a particular landing centre. For reference, the published Thane / Palghar values include Pomfret ₹646,693/tonne; black pomfrets ₹463,215/tonne; seer fish ₹465,785/tonne; prawns ₹239,787/tonne; mackerels ₹126,139/tonne; and Bombil ₹89,791/tonne. The page converts these to approximate rupees per kg by dividing by 1,000.

These annual boatside averages are historical wholesale context. They are not retail prices, today's auction prices, prices for one market, or a forecast. Do not imply a trend unless comparing the same category, unit and geographic coverage across years.

For a more recent sampled snapshot at selected harbours, check ICAR-CMFRI FishWatch: https://www.cmfri.org.in/fishwatch. FishWatch landing and auction values are based on sampled craft and its harbour coverage may not include Palghar. State the source harbour and date and never substitute another harbour's quote for a Palghar price.

Assistant guidance: when asked for a current Palghar fish price, say that this reference library has no verified live Palghar-specific quote. Offer the annual Thane / Palghar figure only when clearly labeled with the species, year, unit and combined district geography.

Title: How to interpret weather forecasts and official warnings
Source: Open-Meteo Forecast API documentation
URL: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs
Terms: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms
Checked: 2026-11-04

Open-Meteo provides current and forecast weather-model variables such as temperature, precipitation, precipitation probability, wind, gusts, and pressure. The forecast is model guidance. A rainfall or wind forecast is not an official disaster warning, a flood forecast, or proof that a hazard will occur at a particular place.

For India, check active government warnings through the National Disaster Management Authority's SACHET portal and the India Meteorological Department. SACHET is a national alert portal that disseminates warnings from agencies including IMD, CWC, INCOIS, DGRE, FSI, and state disaster management authorities.

The dashboard checks the public IMD English CAP RSS feed at https://cap-sources.s3.amazonaws.com/in-imd-en/rss.xml. Feed status and publication time are displayed separately from forecast data. The NDMA SACHET portal (https://sachet.ndma.gov.in/) and IMD Mausam (https://mausam.imd.gov.in/) are official places to confirm active warnings.

Coverage note

These are dated studies and regional satellite products. They do not show today’s beach water safety, confirm a new pollution incident, or replace laboratory testing.

WATER QUALITYSamples · 2015–2016

Dahanu and Tarapur coastal transects

The MPCB study reported Dahanu coastal water quality index values of 71–76. Tarapur’s coastal and creek results were described as good post-monsoon, while the creek ranged from bad to medium pre-monsoon.

Historical sampling; this is not a current bathing-water result.

Read MPCB coastal monitoring report ↗
SHORELINE CHANGE1990–2016 assessment

Erosion noted at Bordi and Kelva

NCCR’s assessment lists Bordi, Kelva, and Shrigaon among Maharashtra beaches observed with erosion. Its shoreline-length table groups Palghar with Thane, so the district-group totals cannot be read as Palghar-only measurements.

Historical satellite analysis; no live erosion tracking.

Read NCCR shoreline assessment ↗
MANGROVESAssessment · 2023

Palghar’s share of Maharashtra cover

The Maharashtra Economic Survey reports state mangrove cover of 315.09 km² for 2023; Palghar accounted for 12.2% of the state’s mangrove-cover distribution in that assessment.

District-level share, not a village-by-village map.

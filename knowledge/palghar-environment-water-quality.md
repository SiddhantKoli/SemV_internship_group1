GEOGRAPHIC AND TIME COVERAGE
The report includes coastal transect sampling off Dahanu and Tarapur, with pre-monsoon and post-monsoon samples collected during 2015–2016. This note records only those named locations and the study period. It does not provide a current measurement for every Palghar beach, village, creek, or household water source.

REPORTED RESULTS
- Dahanu coastal transect: the report gives water quality index (WQI) values in the range 71–76.
- Tarapur coastal and creek samples: described as good in the post-monsoon period.
- Tarapur creek: described as bad to medium in the pre-monsoon period.

HOW TO INTERPRET THIS
These are historical study findings from 2015–2016. They cannot establish today's water quality, a current pollution incident, or whether a named beach is safe for bathing. A WQI summary is not a beach-safety certification and should not be treated as a direct chemical concentration. Do not generalize the sampled transects to unsampled villages or to present-day conditions.

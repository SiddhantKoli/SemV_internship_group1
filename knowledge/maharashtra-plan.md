The Maharashtra State Disaster Management Plan provides the state-level framework for preparedness, mitigation, response, and recovery. Its published hazard discussion includes cyclones and their effects in coastal districts, flooding, landslides, drought, heat waves, and other hazards. Palghar is a coastal Konkan district and appears in the plan's historical cyclone impact tables.

This state plan is an operational reference, not a real-time warning or a quantitative forecast. For district-specific procedures, prioritize the latest Palghar District Disaster Management Plan and district notices.

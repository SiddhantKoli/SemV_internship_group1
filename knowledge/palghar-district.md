Title: Palghar district disaster information and official contacts
Source: District Disaster Management, District Palghar, Government of Maharashtra
URL: https://palghar.gov.in/en/disaster-management/
Checked: 2026-11-04

The Palghar district administration's Disaster Management page lists the District Disaster Management Plan 2025-26, a flood SOP, a disasters do's and don'ts booklet, and the Palghar Heat Action Plan 2026. The documents are official references; consult the linked current document for detailed procedures and any revisions.

Published control-room contacts on the district page: Palghar District Disaster Management control room 02525-297474 and toll-free 1077; Maharashtra Disaster Management control room 022-22027990; National Disaster Management Authority control room 011-26701728. In an immediate emergency, contact the relevant official control room or India's emergency number 112. Verify phone numbers on the official page before relying on them during an incident.

The district page is a source directory. A listed plan or hotline is not itself a live incident alert. The assistant must distinguish the district's published preparedness material from current official warnings and from forecast-model output.

Title: Fish market prices, FishWatch coverage, and Palghar historical data
Source: ICAR-CMFRI FishWatch; Maharashtra Directorate of Economics and Statistics
URL: https://www.cmfri.org.in/fishwatch
Checked: 2026-11-05

CMFRI FishWatch publishes near-real-time estimates of selected marine fish landings and auction price ranges at selected fisheries harbours. Its page describes landing quantities as estimates from sampled fishing craft over a 24-hour period, from noon on one day to noon the next. Price values are auction ranges in rupees per kilogram. Always identify the harbour and date printed by FishWatch. Do not assume its selected-harbour list includes Palghar, and do not present another harbour's price as a Palghar quote.

FishWatch source and data-use note: https://www.cmfri.org.in/fishwatch
CMFRI FishWatch data is based on observations from sampled craft at selected harbours. It is not a guaranteed census of all arrivals or a retail-price survey. CMFRI requests source acknowledgement when the information is reused.

The Maharashtra Statistical Abstract 2022-23 has a table of average annual wholesale (boatside) prices by marine fish variety. The district column combines Thane / Palghar. Treat it as an annual historical reference, not a current local market price. Table prices are reported in rupees per tonne.

Selected exact Thane / Palghar combined values from Statistical Abstract 2022-23 Table 16.6: Pomfret ₹646,693/tonne; black pomfrets ₹463,215/tonne; seer fish ₹465,785/tonne; prawns ₹239,787/tonne; mackerels ₹126,139/tonne; Bombil ₹89,791/tonne. More details and the original unit/geographic caveat are in palghar-fish-market-history.md.

Assistant rules: quote numeric prices only when the exact figure is present in retrieved source material. For every price, retain the source, harbour or district group, date/year, unit, and whether it is sampled auction or annual boatside data. Do not convert an annual Thane/Palghar figure into a current Palghar rate, compare unlike units as if equivalent, or forecast prices. If the requested market or date is missing, state that no matching price is available and point to the official source or local market.

Source: National Centre for Coastal Research · national assessment published 2018

GEOGRAPHIC AND TIME COVERAGE
The NCCR assessment analyzes shoreline change over 1990–2016. It names Bordi, Kelva, and Shrigaon among Maharashtra beaches observed with erosion. The coast-length table reports a combined “Palghar & Thane” grouping of 126.64 km. That grouping must not be described as Palghar-only.

REPORTED LENGTHS IN THE PALGHAR & THANE GROUP
- High erosion: 1.12 km
- Moderate erosion: 4.98 km
- Low erosion: 61.66 km
- Stable: 48.44 km
- Low accretion: 6.28 km
- Moderate accretion: 2.26 km
- High accretion: 1.90 km

HOW TO INTERPRET THIS
These are historical shoreline classifications, not current erosion rates or a forecast. The combined coast-length totals cannot be assigned to Palghar district alone. Naming a beach in the assessment does not establish a present-day threat at a specific property, road, or settlement. Do not infer causes or future impacts from these totals alone.

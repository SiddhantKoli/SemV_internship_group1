Title: Maharashtra mangrove cover assessment and Palghar district share
Source: Government of Maharashtra · Economic Survey 2025–26 citing India State of Forest Report 2023
Document: Economic Survey of Maharashtra · Environment chapter
URL: https://mahades.maharashtra.gov.in/ESM1920/chapter/English/ch7_e.pdf
Checked: 2026-11-06

ASSESSMENT YEAR AND COVERAGE
The cited assessment year is 2023. The Maharashtra Economic Survey reports statewide mangrove cover of 315.09 square kilometres and gives Palghar a 12.2% share in the state's district distribution.

HOW TO INTERPRET THIS
The statewide total and district share are assessment statistics, not a live mangrove map. The 12.2% figure is a share of the Maharashtra district distribution; it is not a village-level estimate, a measure of current loss or gain, or proof of the condition of a particular creek. Avoid presenting it as a recent local change unless a newer, spatially detailed assessment is available.

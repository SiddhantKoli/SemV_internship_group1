Title: Coastal hazards and mitigation in the Konkan region
Source: Konkan Disaster Mitigation Project, Maharashtra State Disaster Management Authority
URL: https://sdma.maharashtra.gov.in/en/konkan-disaster-mitigation-project/
Checked: 2026-11-04

The Maharashtra State Disaster Management Authority describes cyclones, floods, high tides of the sea, and landslides among the hazards addressed by the Konkan Disaster Mitigation Project. Palghar is one of the five coastal Konkan districts in the project. The programme includes structural measures such as saline embankments, anti-sea-erosion bunds, underground cabling, multipurpose cyclone shelters, and landslide-protection works.

These are district- and region-level hazard categories. They do not indicate that a particular event is happening today or that a specific village is currently at risk. A location-specific conclusion needs current official warnings and suitable local exposure data.

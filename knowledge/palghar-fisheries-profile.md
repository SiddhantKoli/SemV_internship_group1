Title: Palghar district fisheries profile and landing centres
Source: District Strategic Plan for Palghar 2023, using Fisheries Department and Directorate of Economics and Statistics figures
URL: https://abhinavpahal.nic.in/visions_doc/489VBcyu4MPalgharDSP1July2024.pdf
Checked: 2026-11-05

The Palghar District Strategic Plan reports 85 km of coastline, 74 fishing villages, 18,203 active fishers and 24 fish landing centres. Its fisheries production series is 43,220 metric tonnes in 2020-21, 25,252 metric tonnes in 2021-22, and 43,069 metric tonnes in 2022-23. These are historical district-level figures, not today's catch or an estimate for one harbour.

The plan names landing/fishing centres in Palghar taluka: Satpati, Dativare, Murabe, Navapur, Dandi, Alevadi and Nandgaon. It names Naigaon, Pacu Port, Castle Harbour and Arnala in Vasai taluka, and Bordi, Chinchani and Dahanu in Dahanu taluka. The plan says there are 24 landing centres overall but its named list is not exhaustive. Use names as the plan spells them; do not infer exact coordinates or current facility status from this list.

The plan does not provide separate Virar-level landings, catch, or price statistics. Do not describe Vasai-taluka numbers as Virar-specific.

Fish varieties mentioned in the plan include Pomfret, Surmai (seer fish), Rohu, Katla, Bombil, Mrugal, Javla and prawns. This is a district profile, not proof of what is available at a given landing centre or season.

The plan gives different boat counts in its narrative and table: narrative text says approximately 2,030 vessels (1,999 mechanized and 31 non-mechanized), while a table lists 1,999 registered boats. Do not present a single reconciled boat count without checking with the district fisheries office.

Assistant guidance: answer Palghar questions from this source with the year and district scope attached. Distinguish the Palghar area from Vasai/Virar and from combined Thane/Palghar datasets. Never state current catch, today's availability, exact village coordinates, or live fishery conditions from this historical plan.

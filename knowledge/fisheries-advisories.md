Title: Official fisheries advisories and Palghar coverage notes
Source: INCOIS Potential Fishing Zone Advisory; Maharashtra Department of Fisheries; ICAR-CMFRI
URL: https://www.incois.gov.in/MarineFisheries/PfzAdvisory
Checked: 2026-11-05

INCOIS issues Potential Fishing Zone (PFZ) advisories for the Maharashtra sector as maps and text. The official service describes the advisories as potential zones of fish aggregation identified with remotely sensed ocean information including sea-surface temperature and chlorophyll. The public advisory page states that the service is issued daily for fishing landing centres when operating, but can pause during government fishing bans or adverse sea-state conditions. Read the forecast date and validity on the current official advisory before using it.

A PFZ is an advisory to help search for potential fish aggregation. It does not guarantee a catch, report the quantity or species of fish at a point, or certify that it is safe to go to sea. The marked zone may shift. Do not turn an old map or archived advisory into a current location recommendation.

The Maharashtra Fisheries Department provides a fish-landing-centres page. It is a directory/map source, not a live fish-price feed: https://fisheries.maharashtra.gov.in/fish-landing-centers/

ICAR-CMFRI estimates fish landings using sampled craft and publishes data by state/zone, species, gear, and time period. Coverage and spatial level vary by dataset; use its own dates and geographic labels. Requests for specific fisheries data can be directed to CMFRI: https://www.cmfri.org.in/fish-catch-estimates

Palghar district baseline and its named landing centres are summarized separately in palghar-fisheries-profile.md from the District Strategic Plan for Palghar: https://abhinavpahal.nic.in/visions_doc/489VBcyu4MPalgharDSP1July2024.pdf. This is historical district information, not a current fishing advisory.

When asked about today's advisory, link to the current INCOIS map/text and say to check its validity. Never describe PFZ as a catch guarantee or a safety clearance.
